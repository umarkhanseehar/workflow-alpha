FAQ

Before you get in touch

What does Workflow Alpha actually do?

We build and manage the system behind your professional presence: positioning, LinkedIn profile optimization, content creation and repurposing, publishing and distribution, and, when agreed, lead-generation and follow-up support. Umar Khan leads the strategy and works with Sufian Dildar, who handles web development and AI automation.

Do I need existing content to start?

No. If you already have videos, podcasts, webinars, articles or reports, we turn them into a consistent content system. If you have expertise but no content library, we build from short voice notes, written answers and discovery sessions.

How much time will this take from me?

We design the workflow to minimise meetings and use asynchronous communication where practical. A typical input schedule is two or three short voice notes per week (usually two to five minutes each), plus reviewing and approving drafts. The exact schedule is agreed with you.

Will the content sound like me?

That is the aim. Drafts are written from your own voice notes, opinions, experience and source material, then sent to you for review. Nothing is published without your approval.

Do you guarantee leads, clients or growth?

No. We do not guarantee specific results. Outcomes depend on your service, audience, market, timing and sales process. We build the system, publish consistently and review what the available data shows so the process can improve.

Which platforms do you publish on?

LinkedIn is the priority, covering your personal profile and, where relevant, your company page. X, Instagram, Facebook or YouTube can be added according to your needs and the package agreed. Not every platform is included automatically.

Can you edit video for me?

Yes, when you supply suitable footage or agree a suitable recording arrangement. We select useful segments, edit short clips, add captions and formatting, then send them for review. Voice notes alone do not create video footage.

Do you do outbound outreach?

Where it is part of the agreed scope. That can include target-audience definition, prospect research, personalised outreach and follow-up. We use professional practices and respect the rules of each channel. We do not do mass spam or artificial engagement.

Is this an AI automation agency?

No. AI can support research, drafting and workflow efficiency, but our core value is a managed personal brand growth system led by people, not software, with you approving what goes out.

Do you have client case studies?

Workflow Alpha is building its portfolio. The projects on our Portfolio page are clearly labelled demonstration projects. We will add verified client work only once it exists and the client has agreed.

How do I get started?

Request a Personal Brand Review through the contact page. Tell us about your profession and where you are starting from, and we will recommend the most suitable path.
