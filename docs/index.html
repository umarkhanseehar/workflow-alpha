Quick answers

Good to know

What does Workflow Alpha actually do?

We build and manage the system behind your professional presence: positioning, LinkedIn profile optimization, content creation and repurposing, publishing and distribution, and, when agreed, lead-generation and follow-up support. Umar Khan leads the strategy and works with Sufian Dildar, who handles web development and AI automation.

Do I need existing content to start?

No. If you already have videos, podcasts, webinars, articles or reports, we turn them into a consistent content system. If you have expertise but no content library, we build from short voice notes, written answers and discovery sessions.

How much time will this take from me?

We design the workflow to minimise meetings and use asynchronous communication where practical. A typical input schedule is two or three short voice notes per week (usually two to five minutes each), plus reviewing and approving drafts. The exact schedule is agreed with you.
