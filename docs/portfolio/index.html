Demonstration projectFictional commercial mortgage broker

One Source, A Week of Content

Source: fictional webinar, “What borrowers should prepare before speaking to a lender”

MonLinkedIn post

The three documents that make a first lender conversation easier.

TueCarousel

5 slides: what to prepare before you approach a lender.

WedNewsletter idea

A short note answering the question borrowers ask most.

ThuShort-video script

30-second script from the clearest segment of the recording.

FriArticle outline

A longer piece expanding Monday's post.

The problem

A useful 30-minute webinar was recorded once, then forgotten. The expertise inside it never reached the audience again.

The process

  1. Review the recording and mark the strongest ideas
  2. Extract insights, opinions and audience questions
  3. Assign each idea to the format that suits it
  4. Draft for review and approval

Intended purpose

Shows how one existing source becomes several adapted assets, rather than the same text pasted everywhere.

Demonstration projectFictional executive coach

Voice Notes to a Personal Brand Content Plan

Sample voice note (3 min): “Leaders keep asking me how to give feedback without damaging trust...”

Feedback & trust
  • Feedback that lands
  • When silence is the problem
Leading through change
  • What teams need to hear first
  • Small signals that matter
Coaching in practice
  • A question I ask every leader
  • What coaching is not

The problem

Plenty of expertise, no content library, and no time for long writing sessions.

The process

  1. Discovery session to understand expertise, audience and goals
  2. Client sends two or three short voice notes a week
  3. We reply with prompts so the next notes are easy to record
  4. Ideas are organised into pillars and drafted in the client's voice

Intended purpose

Shows how short voice notes provide the raw expertise while we handle the organised content process.

The problem

A profile that lists a job title but does not say who the advisor helps or why they are credible.

The process

  1. Clarify audience and positioning
  2. Rewrite the headline and About section
  3. Recommend Featured items and a clear call to action
  4. Align the profile with the advisor's website

Intended purpose

Shows how a profile can communicate credibility and value more clearly. It does not promise leads.

The problem

Long recordings contain strong moments that are never shared as short, watchable clips.

The process
